# Anthropic

Company file · Arrow Fund Morning Markets · 2026-11-09 (midday TEST edition) · external news only

## Why this company today

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 Monday, its first frontier models since Dario Amodei called for slowing AI development in September, as it readies an IPO that could come as soon as next week. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-claude-fable-5-2-mythos-5-2.html)) The same day, TechCrunch, citing the Financial Times, reported it is buying $3.4 billion of Caterpillar gas turbines capable of up to 1.7 gigawatts. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-joins-ais-natural-gas-frenzy-buys-3-4b-worth-of-turbines/)) CNBC reported investors see it targeting a $1 trillion to $1.5 trillion valuation, down from a $2 trillion target floated in recent months. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/07/anthropic-ipo-ai-data-center-backlash.html))

## Purpose

Build frontier AI models, the Claude family, and sell them primarily to businesses, with an explicit safety-first positioning. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/07/anthropic-ipo-ai-data-center-backlash.html))

## Problem, and who feels it

Enterprises want AI that can do real work, especially agentic coding and complex reasoning. The filing says customers "consistently utilize our latest and most capable models as soon as they are released." ([S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026068676/anthropicpbc-sx1ax1.htm))

## What they sell

Four tiers: Fable (public) and Mythos (vetted life sciences and cyber customers) at the top, then Opus, Sonnet and Haiku. ([Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/anthropic-investors-aren-t-just-buying-a-chatbot-how-the-company-behind-claude-makes-money-ant-stock-ipo-12194852)) Fable 5.2 lists at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, 20% cheaper than 5.1. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-claude-fable-5-2-mythos-5-2.html))

## How they make money

79% of revenue in the first nine months of 2026 was metered token usage ($26.3 billion); subscriptions, including the Claude app, were $6.9 billion. About 44% of business runs through AWS, Google Cloud and Azure marketplaces, which take a cut. ([Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/anthropic-investors-aren-t-just-buying-a-chatbot-how-the-company-behind-claude-makes-money-ant-stock-ipo-12194852))

## Scale we can cite

Revenue of $33.24 billion for the nine months ended Sept. 30, up roughly 1,200%; Q3 revenue of about $17 billion, though sequential growth slowed to about 47% from 144% in Q2. Net loss of $104 billion for the period, mostly from fair-value changes on convertible notes. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/02/anthropic-ant-s-1-quarter-financials.html)) More than 11,900 customers pay $100,000 or more a year, and about 250 pay $10 million or more. ([S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026068676/anthropicpbc-sx1ax1.htm)) The top five customers were about 25% of revenue year to date. ([Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/anthropic-investors-aren-t-just-buying-a-chatbot-how-the-company-behind-claude-makes-money-ant-stock-ipo-12194852))

## Why now, and the catch

Compute. Anthropic disclosed about $895.4 billion of purchase and lease commitments, substantially all compute and infrastructure, with $69.8 billion due in the next 12 months. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/article/anthropic-ipo-what-we-know-143321078.html)) The turbine buy marks a shift from its historic preference to rent capacity. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-joins-ais-natural-gas-frenzy-buys-3-4b-worth-of-turbines/))

## Competition

OpenAI is the chief rival; it filed confidentially in June but signals a 2027 debut. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/02/anthropic-ant-s-1-quarter-financials.html)) It reportedly raised another $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation last week. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/openai-reportedly-raises-an-additional-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation/)) Amazon, Google and Microsoft are simultaneously investors, compute suppliers and competitors. ([Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/anthropic-investors-aren-t-just-buying-a-chatbot-how-the-company-behind-claude-makes-money-ant-stock-ipo-12194852))

## Team

CEO Dario Amodei co-founded the company with researchers who left OpenAI; the company has seven co-founders. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/02/anthropic-ant-s-1-quarter-financials.html)) ([Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/anthropic-investors-aren-t-just-buying-a-chatbot-how-the-company-behind-claude-makes-money-ant-stock-ipo-12194852))
