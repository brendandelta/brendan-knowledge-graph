# Fractile

Company file · Arrow Fund Morning Markets · 2026-11-11 evening edition (for Thursday's Get Smart) · external news only

## Why this company today

Fractile raised a $720 million Series C backed by the U.K.'s Sovereign AI fund. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/with-720m-round-britains-fractile-gets-a-leg-up-in-the-race-for-ai-chips/)) The government says the round, worth over £500 million, values it at over £5 billion. ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/sovereign-ai-and-british-business-bank-invest-in-british-firm-developing-the-next-generation-of-ai-hardware)) It landed on the day Nvidia's board was reported to be weighing Huang's future, a reminder that the market for AI chips has one dominant supplier. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/nvidia-jensen-huang-affair-employee.html))

## Purpose

A "chip-first frontier AI company" building the hardware frontier models run on. ([Fractile](https://www.fractile.ai/))

## Problem, and who feels it

Every generated token requires reading model weights and context from memory, so speed is capped by memory bandwidth, which has scaled far more slowly than compute; chips spend most of their time waiting on data. Long agentic, reasoning and coding workloads make it worse. ([Lightspeed](https://lsvp.com/stories/accelerating-intelligence-our-investment-in-fractile/))

## What they sell, and to whom

Inference accelerators and rack-scale systems built on DRAM stacked directly on top of the logic, which Lightspeed says gives more bandwidth than HBM and more capacity than SRAM designs. ([Lightspeed](https://lsvp.com/stories/accelerating-intelligence-our-investment-in-fractile/)) It also builds a machine-learning compiler. CEO Walter Goodwin says the money lets it "ship [its] first chips and systems next year." ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/with-720m-round-britains-fractile-gets-a-leg-up-in-the-race-for-ai-chips/)) The Information reported in May that Anthropic had discussed buying its chips once available in 2027. ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/fractile-raises-220m-to-accelerate-development-of-ai-inference-chips/))

## Why now

Lightspeed says frontier models process more than 10x more tokens each year and expects roughly 80% of AI chip spend to go to inference. ([Lightspeed](https://lsvp.com/stories/accelerating-intelligence-our-investment-in-fractile/)) London wants home-grown hardware: the Budget added £140 million for Sovereign AI. ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/sovereign-ai-and-british-business-bank-invest-in-british-firm-developing-the-next-generation-of-ai-hardware))

## How they make money

Selling chips and systems. Fractile owns its whole stack rather than outsourcing backend design to Broadcom or another partner, which Lightspeed says gives it more control of costs. No revenue or pricing is public. ([Lightspeed](https://lsvp.com/stories/accelerating-intelligence-our-investment-in-fractile/))

## Competition and alternatives

Nvidia is the incumbent. Among startups, TechCrunch names Axelera, Etched and OLIX as fellow top-funded purpose-built AI chip players. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/with-720m-round-britains-fractile-gets-a-leg-up-in-the-race-for-ai-chips/)) In China, Alibaba is preparing to list its T-Head chip unit. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/alibaba-pr.html))

## Scale we can cite

TechCrunch says the round was led by Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed and Redpoint, and that the FT and a Reuters source put the valuation near $7 billion. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/with-720m-round-britains-fractile-gets-a-leg-up-in-the-race-for-ai-chips/)) The government lists CapitalG, Lightspeed and Redpoint among backers. ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/sovereign-ai-and-british-business-bank-invest-in-british-firm-developing-the-next-generation-of-ai-hardware)) In May it raised a $220 million Series B co-led by Accel, Factorial Funds and Founders Fund, and in February pledged £100 million for U.K. operations over three years. ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/fractile-raises-220m-to-accelerate-development-of-ai-inference-chips/))

## Team

Goodwin founded Fractile in 2022 while a PhD student at Oxford's Robotics Institute; former Intel CEO Pat Gelsinger has invested. It hires in London, Bristol, San Francisco and Taipei. ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/fractile-raises-220m-to-accelerate-development-of-ai-inference-chips/))

## Open question for Get Smart

Fractile pivoted from in-memory compute to stacked DRAM and has not shipped. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/with-720m-round-britains-fractile-gets-a-leg-up-in-the-race-for-ai-chips/)) At about $7 billion pre-product, is this a cleaner way to own the inference shift than Nvidia at 13 times forward earnings, or does sovereign money set a valuation floor that a 2027 tape-out has to grow into?
