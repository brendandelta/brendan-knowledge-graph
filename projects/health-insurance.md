# Move health coverage from Covered California to a Delta plan

**Status:** decide by early November 2026

## Goal
Replace the Covered California individual plan with a higher-end policy through Delta Meridian, with no coverage gap and no surprise at tax time.

## Done when
Delta plan is effective, Covered CA is cancelled effective the day before, premium tax credits (if any) stopped the same month, and the broker's paperwork is filed in the Delta Drive.

## Dates (verify against coveredca.com before relying on them)
- Covered CA open enrollment for 2027: Nov 1, 2026 to Jan 31, 2027. Doing nothing auto-renews into a 2027 plan.
- To switch cleanly on Jan 1: Delta plan bound and Covered CA cancelled by mid-December.
- You can leave Covered CA any month once employer coverage exists; cancellation is effective end of month, roughly two weeks' notice.
- Small-group plan or ICHRA for a one-employee S-corp: broker needed, several weeks of paperwork, starts on the 1st of a month.
- Premium tax credits taken in 2026 reconcile on the 2026 return. Stop them the month Delta coverage starts.

## Decisions
- [ ] Route: small-group plan through Delta, or ICHRA (Delta reimburses an individual premium pre-tax). Ask the broker and the accountant which is cheaper after tax.

## Steps
- [ ] Early Nov: pick route; engage a broker (ask Yeyson or the Arrow bookkeepers for a referral).
- [ ] Mid Nov: quotes in hand; choose plan; set effective date (Dec 1 or Jan 1).
- [ ] On binding: cancel Covered CA effective the day before; stop any premium tax credit.
- [ ] File everything in Delta Drive; add the premium to the Delta books as a deductible expense (accounting-and-tax).
- [ ] chief-of-staff: raise this project the first week of November and weekly until done.
