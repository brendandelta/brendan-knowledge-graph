# Nscale

Company file · Arrow Fund Morning Markets · 2026-11-12 evening edition (for Friday's Get Smart) · external news only

## Why this company today

Nscale filed an amended IPO registration with the SEC on Thursday, adding preliminary nine-month results. ([SEC S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm)) It landed the day Nvidia, one of its backers, lost its CEO and rival Nebius fell 16%. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nscale-updates-ipo-prospectus-that-shows-widening-loss.html))

## Purpose

In its own words, "a full-stack AI hyperscaler" that owns everything from powered land and on-site generation to data centers, GPU fleets and the cloud software on top. ([SEC S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm))

## Problem, and who feels it

The filing calls access to large, reliable power "the primary gating factor" for AI infrastructure. Frontier labs and hyperscalers that need multi-gigawatt capacity feel it most, and Nscale has built its footprint in low-cost, renewable-rich power regions such as Norway, Portugal and Iceland. ([SEC S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm))

## What they sell, and to whom

GPU compute under multi-year take-or-pay contracts, plus Nscale Cloud for inference, fine-tuning and managed Kubernetes and Slurm. ([Nscale](https://www.nscale.com/)) Microsoft statements of work provide for up to about $43.8 billion through 2033, and August agreements with Anthropic provide for up to about $44.6 billion, both subject to delivery requirements. ([SEC S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm))

## Why now

In March it bought AIPCorp and its roughly 2,250-acre Monarch campus in West Virginia, with an initial 2 GW of gross power expected online in the first half of 2028 and about 8 GW planned by 2031. On Sept. 29, Anthropic exercised its right to contract for a second building there. It also agreed in July to buy Anyscale, the company behind the open-source Ray framework, which the filing says has 740 million cumulative downloads. ([SEC S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm))

## How they make money

Long-term contracts with a weighted average life of about 5.7 years. Contracted value has jumped: about $105.8 billion active and contracted, $6.4 billion of it active, versus $38.0 billion at the end of 2025. ([SEC S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm))

## Competition and alternatives

The filing names CoreWeave, Nebius, Crusoe and Lambda, plus SpaceX as a new large-scale compute provider, and the general-purpose clouds. ([SEC S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm))

## Scale we can cite

About 50,000 active GPUs and 461,000 active and contracted, across about 1.37 GW of active and contracted capacity. Preliminary nine-month revenue was $307.5 million versus $21.3 million a year earlier, with a net loss of $1.69 billion and adjusted EBITDA of negative $289.5 million. It has raised over $3.3 billion in series financing plus billions in GPU and site debt facilities. Its largest customer was 52% of first-half revenue. ([SEC S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm))

## Team

Founder Josh Payne is CEO and board chair. Nscale was spun out of Arkon Energy in May 2024. ([SEC S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm))

## Open question for Get Smart

A $3.22 billion Microsoft work order was terminated after the power connection at its Loughton, U.K. site was withdrawn, ([SEC S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm)) and the FT reported Microsoft will not renew a Texas contract. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nscale-updates-ipo-prospectus-that-shows-widening-loss.html)) With most of the backlog in two customers and GPU lenders now asking for more after Huang's exit, is $105.8 billion of contracts worth more than the power that has to arrive by 2028?
