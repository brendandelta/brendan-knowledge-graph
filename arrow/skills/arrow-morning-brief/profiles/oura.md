# Oura

Company file · Arrow Fund Morning Markets · 2026-11-10 evening edition (for Wednesday's Get Smart) · external news only

## Why this company today

Oura agreed Tuesday to buy Counsel Health, an AI-native primary care company, for $625 million in cash, and disclosed preliminary results for its fiscal year ended Sept. 30. ([Counsel Health / Oura release](https://www.counselhealth.com/newsroom/oura-to-acquire-counsel-health-advancing-vision-for-more-connected-personalized-healthcare)) It comes weeks after Oura postponed an IPO of up to $2.2 billion, citing "uncertainty in the IPO market," on the same day Anthropic set its own IPO terms. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/oura-to-acquire-ai-native-primary-care-startup-counsel-health-for-625m/))

## Purpose

A health intelligence platform, built around the smart ring, that turns continuous biometric data into personalized insight and, with Counsel, into care. ([Release](https://www.counselhealth.com/newsroom/oura-to-acquire-counsel-health-advancing-vision-for-more-connected-personalized-healthcare))

## Problem, and who feels it

Consumers who track their health still struggle to get timely care. CEO Tom Hale points to "higher healthcare costs, a shortage of doctors, and limited time" in doctor visits. ([Oura](https://ouraring.com/blog/oura-to-acquire-counsel-health/))

## What they sell, and to whom

The Oura Ring plus a membership at $5.99 a month that unlocks more than 50 health metrics and Oura Advisor, its AI health companion. ([Oura](https://ouraring.com/membership)) Oura says it has more than six million members. Counsel adds medical AI with escalation to a board-certified physician in minutes inside the Oura app, and keeps selling to health plans, health systems and employers. ([Release](https://www.counselhealth.com/newsroom/oura-to-acquire-counsel-health-advancing-vision-for-more-connected-personalized-healthcare))

## Why now

Counsel has been integrated into the Oura app since a partnership began in May, and more than half of Counsel users in Oura Labs have shared their Oura data with physicians. ([Oura](https://ouraring.com/blog/oura-to-acquire-counsel-health/)) Executive chairman David Shuman says the deal expands the addressable market and lifetime value, and opens the door to people beyond the member base. ([Release](https://www.counselhealth.com/newsroom/oura-to-acquire-counsel-health-advancing-vision-for-more-connected-personalized-healthcare))

## How they make money

Ring sales plus recurring membership. Fortune, citing IPOX's Kat Liu, noted sales still lean heavily toward hardware, which is why the valuation hinges on whether investors see a ring maker or a digital-health platform. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/06/why-did-oura-pull-its-ipo/))

## Competition and alternatives

Ring rivals RingConn and Ultrahuman. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/oura-to-acquire-ai-native-primary-care-startup-counsel-health-for-625m/)) In wider wearables, Apple, Google, Garmin, Fitbit and Whoop; D.A. Davidson's Gil Luria told Fortune that if Apple builds a ring, Oura's business "doesn't need to exist." ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/06/why-did-oura-pull-its-ipo/))

## Scale we can cite

Fiscal fourth-quarter revenue grew 153% to $533 million with $87 million of adjusted EBITDA, a 16% margin; fiscal 2026 revenue was about $1.75 billion, up 92%. Results are preliminary and unaudited. ([Release](https://www.counselhealth.com/newsroom/oura-to-acquire-counsel-health-advancing-vision-for-more-connected-personalized-healthcare)) It was valued at $11 billion after a $900 million round in October 2025. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/oura-to-acquire-ai-native-primary-care-startup-counsel-health-for-625m/)) The shelved IPO marketed 50 million shares at $40 to $44, up to $15.6 billion fully diluted, with 73% of shares from selling holders. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/06/why-did-oura-pull-its-ipo/))

## Team

Tom Hale is CEO and David Shuman executive chairman. ([Release](https://www.counselhealth.com/newsroom/oura-to-acquire-counsel-health-advancing-vision-for-more-connected-personalized-healthcare)) Counsel founder Muthu Alagappan, MD, joins as chief clinical AI officer with Counsel's roughly 70 employees, per Bloomberg via TechCrunch. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/oura-to-acquire-ai-native-primary-care-startup-counsel-health-for-625m/))
