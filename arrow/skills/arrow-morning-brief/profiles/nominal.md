# Nominal

Company file · Arrow Fund Morning Markets · 2026-11-09 evening edition (for Tuesday's Get Smart) · external news only

## Why this company today

Nominal announced a $240 million Series C at a $3 billion valuation Monday, led by Lightspeed and co-led by Insight Partners. ([Nominal](https://nominal.io/blog/series-c-news)) That triples the $1 billion valuation of its March round, a preemptive Series B extension led by Trae Stephens at Founders Fund. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/ai-hardware-startup-nominal-triples-valuation-to-3b-in-8-months/)) It lands the same day the market is debating the AI build-out from the power, permitting and defense side.

## Purpose

An intelligence platform that keeps everything a team knows about its machines in one place, so engineers and their AI agents can build hardware faster. ([Nominal](https://nominal.io/about))

## Problem, and who feels it

Engineers building aircraft, spacecraft, reactors and factories work with data scattered across legacy systems and knowledge that lives in individual heads. ([Nominal](https://nominal.io/blog/series-c-news)) Lightspeed describes test data copied to USB drives and parsed by Python scripts only one engineer understands, and estimates roughly 600,000 engineers behind most U.S. R&D, whose labor adds up to about $100 billion a year. ([Lightspeed](https://lsvp.com/stories/hardware-engineering-gets-its-intelligence-layer/))

## What they sell, and to whom

A platform that aligns telemetry, video, logs and specs on one timeline, plus Nominal Analyst, an AI agent whose answers trace back to the underlying evidence. It runs in the cloud, on premises, air-gapped or at the edge. More than 80 commercial and government organizations use it, including five of the seven largest defense contractors. ([Nominal](https://nominal.io/blog/series-c-news)) At Anduril, more than 300 engineers use it, and analysis that took five to six hours now happens in near real time. ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nominal-series-c-240m-3b-valuation-hardware-ai))

## Why now

Reindustrialization, reshoring and physical AI are raising the stakes for hardware teams, and general-purpose models lack an industry's proprietary data and context, per the company and Insight. ([Nominal](https://nominal.io/blog/series-c-news)) The Air Force selected Nominal in October for a $71.2 million STRATFI award, a program that requires outside matching funds. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/ai-hardware-startup-nominal-triples-valuation-to-3b-in-8-months/))

## How they make money

Software sold to commercial and government engineering organizations; pricing was not disclosed in today's coverage. The company told Bloomberg its revenue has more than tripled so far this year, which Bloomberg tied to the defense tech spending boom. ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nominal-series-c-240m-3b-valuation-hardware-ai))

## Competition and alternatives

The main incumbent is the status quo: file servers, CSVs, spreadsheets and test software Lightspeed says was written during the Cold War. Lightspeed frames the opportunity as the hardware version of vertical AI plays like Harvey in legal and Abridge in healthcare. ([Lightspeed](https://lsvp.com/stories/hardware-engineering-gets-its-intelligence-layer/)) No named direct competitor is cited in today's coverage.

## Scale we can cite

$3 billion valuation, $240 million Series C, more than 80 customers. ([Nominal](https://nominal.io/blog/series-c-news)) Other backers include Sequoia, General Catalyst, Lux Capital and Founders Fund. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/ai-hardware-startup-nominal-triples-valuation-to-3b-in-8-months/)) This year it bought Fid Labs, whose team builds its agents, and Pull Systems, which takes it into automotive. ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nominal-series-c-240m-3b-valuation-hardware-ai))

## Team

Founded in 2022 by Cameron McCord, a former Navy nuclear submarine officer who worked at Anduril and Saildrone; Bryce Strauss, from Lockheed space programs; and Jason Hoch, from Palantir Foundry and Vercel. ([Lightspeed](https://lsvp.com/stories/hardware-engineering-gets-its-intelligence-layer/)) Headquartered in Austin with offices in Los Angeles, New York, Seattle, Washington, D.C. and London. ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nominal-series-c-240m-3b-valuation-hardware-ai))
