## 4. The $8,000 double-count (found & fixed 8/4)
Invoice #1006 ($8,000 "services for June") + a manufactured 6/15 payment into Business Intake, while the real 6/30 $8,000 wire was also booked. Proven three ways: (1) real deposits account for every dollar — no 11th payment exists; (2) QBO showed Intake at $8,277.46 vs. real $277.46; (3) after deletion every account tied to the penny. Fix: deleted payment then invoice. **Lesson:** never trust an invoice + payment pair without a matching real deposit; always tie to live bank balances.
